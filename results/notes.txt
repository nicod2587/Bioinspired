results, 27 nov. everything here is from the current code on main, so use these numbers for the slides
all runs: 500 m cells (100x100), 5 min steps, wind 8 m/s, density on, P0 0.04, wind C1 0.15 / C2 0.2
"hours to town" = first time any town cell catches fire. 168 h (a week) = gave up, never got there
each csv has one row per run so you can make your own charts / tables for the appendix

Q1 - power plant vs incinerator, north wind      (py experiments.py)
  wind_sweep.csv / .png   10 runs per direction, both ignitions
  north wind: incinerator 28.2 +- 1.3 h, power plant 38.8 +- 3.0 h (mean)
  the 30 run medians on nico's slide (27.7 / 37.3 h) are the "none" rows in forest_extension.csv
  so the incinerator gets there about 10 h (a quarter) sooner

Q2 - wind direction sweep, incinerator              (same files as Q1)
  mean hours (runs that got there out of 10)
    wind from   incinerator        power plant
    N           28.2  (10/10)      38.8  (10/10)
    NE          28.1  (10/10)      123.2 (9/10)
    E           51.0  (10/10)      never
    SE          161.8 (1/10)       never
    S, SW       never              never
    W           never              129.4 (10/10)
    NW          53.4  (10/10)      38.0  (10/10)
  worst for the incinerator is N or NE. any wind with a south part blows it away from the town

wind speed                                         (py experiments.py 10 <speed>)
  wind_sweep_0ms / _5ms / _15ms .csv / .png, same as above at other speeds (8 m/s is wind_sweep.csv)
  north wind, mean hours:
    speed      incinerator        power plant
    0 m/s      47.6  (9/10)       66.3  (10/10)
    5 m/s      34.4               47.1
    8 m/s      28.2               38.8
    15 m/s     18.7               25.3
  at 15 m/s the power plant fire only reaches the town with N or NW wind

Q3 - water drop, 50 cells = 12.5 km2               (py water_drop.py)
  water_tactics.csv / .png   incinerator fire, north wind, 30 runs each
  planes drop 2 cells an hour from hour 1. wet 1 h then damp 23 h, a fire next to it dries it faster
    tactic                         median h   change
    no drop                        27.7
    early attack (near incinerator) 28.0      +0.3
    ring round the town            28.7       +1.0
    follow the head of the fire    30.7       +3.0  (2/30 runs never reached the town, very spread out)
    line from forest to lake       32.1       +4.4
  "ring dropped last" (36.8 h) and "canyon plugs then ring" (37.8 h) on nico's slide were
  separate runs, they're not in the csv
  water_drop_scan_instant.png / water_scan_instant.npy - every position of a line and a square,
  OLD: all water dropped at once at the start, before the planes/drying were added. only use as background
